1. Introduction

Welcome to Tap Away Arrows by ALFA Labs ("we", "our", or "the Game"). We are committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of our players. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you download, install, and play Tap Away Arrows by ALFA Labs on your Android device.

2. Information We Collect

To provide seamless cross-device cloud synchronization and secure game progress backup, our game utilizes Firebase Authentication and Google Cloud Firestore.

A. Account & Authentication Data

When you sign in using Google Sign-In or Email/Password via Firebase Authentication, we collect:

  • Your email address and display name
  • A secure unique User ID (UID) generated by Firebase
B. Cloud Gameplay & Sync Data (Firebase Firestore)

Your game progress and virtual inventory are securely stored and synced to Google Cloud Firestore. This includes:

  • Completed levels, stars, and high scores
  • Golden Coin balance and purchase history state
  • Game settings (audio volume, sound effects toggle, and haptic preferences)
C. Google Play In-App Purchases

The game offers optional in-app purchases for Golden Coins via the official Google Play Billing Library.

  • All financial transactions and billing details are handled directly and securely by Google Play. We never receive or store credit card numbers.
  • We receive cryptographic verification tokens from Google Play to credit virtual coins.

3. Permissions Requested

Tap Away Arrows requests minimal permissions necessary for gameplay:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required for Firebase Cloud Sync, Google Sign-In, and Google Play Billing verification.
  • VIBRATE: Used for haptic tactile feedback.

4. Third-Party Services

We use trusted cloud services provided by Google LLC:

  • Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore: For account management and cloud save synchronization.
  • Google Play Services & Billing: For secure in-app purchases.
  • Google Privacy Policy

5. Children's Privacy

Tap Away Arrows is a family-friendly puzzle game. While general audiences enjoy the game, users who sign in with Google or email provide account data. We encourage parents and guardians to supervise children's online activities.

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

You maintain full control over your data and account at all times:

  • In-App Deletion: You can permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data instantly within the game by navigating to Settings > Account > Delete Account.
  • Web Deletion Request: You can also request account and data deletion by visiting our Account Deletion Portal or emailing our support team.

7. Security

All data transmitted between the app and Firebase Cloud Firestore is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS protocols and secured via strict zero-trust Firestore Security Rules.
